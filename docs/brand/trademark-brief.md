# Brief for a trademark lawyer: "Upleaf"

Prepared 14 November 2026. This is what a preliminary search found, set out so a trademark professional can give an opinion without repeating it. It is not legal advice and not a clearance.

## What is being asked

1. Can **Upleaf** be used and registered in **Thailand** for a study app, in Nice classes **9** (downloadable software), **41** (education) and **42** (software as a service)?
2. Is the Swiss mark **UpLeap** (below) an obstacle, in Thailand or if the app is later offered elsewhere?
3. Should the application cover the word alone, the logo, or both?

## The product

A mobile-first web app for university students in Thailand (Thai and English): class timetable and exam calendar, notes, quizzes, flashcards, mock exams, a degree planner and reminders. The name is shown in Latin letters only. The logo is an open book whose right-hand page lifts like a leaf, in leaf green (`#15803d`) and lime (`#a3e635`); files are in this folder.

Not yet launched under this name. No domain registered: `upleaf.com` belongs to a web agency, `upleaf.app` did not resolve.

## What was searched, and what was not

Searched: Thailand's DIP register (search.ipthailand.go.th), by word and Thai transliterations; WIPO Global Brand Database, worldwide and filtered to Thailand, including fuzzy and phonetic matching.

Not searched: **TMview** (it refuses automated access; a person can run it at https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/#/tmview/results?page=1&pageSize=30&criteria=C&basicSearch=upleaf), **ASEAN TMview** (unreachable on the day). National offices other than Thailand's were seen only through WIPO's copy of their data, unless the section below says otherwise. Only word marks were compared, not logos.

Records open by number at `https://search.ipthailand.go.th/search?q=<number>&tab=trademark`; the Indonesian record is at https://branddb.wipo.int/en/similarname/brand/ID502023DID110042.
