# Brief for a trademark lawyer: "Termtrail"

Prepared 14 November 2026. This sets out what a preliminary search found, so a trademark professional can give an opinion without repeating it. It is not legal advice and not a clearance.

## What is being asked

1. Can **Termtrail** be used and registered in **Thailand** for a study app, in Nice classes **9** (downloadable software), **41** (education) and **42** (software as a service)?
2. Does the unregistered "TermTrail" freelancer tool (below) matter, in Thailand or if the app is later offered elsewhere?
3. Should the application cover the word alone, the logo, or both? Should a Thai transliteration (เทอมเทรล) be filed as well?

## The product

A mobile-first web app for university students in Thailand (Thai and English): class timetable and exam calendar, notes, quizzes, flashcards, mock exams, a degree planner and reminders. The name is shown in Latin letters. The logo is an open book whose right-hand page lifts into a tick, in indigo with an amber page; files are in this folder.

Not yet launched under this name. No domain is registered yet: `termtrail.com`, `termtrail.app` and `termtrail.co.th` were all unregistered on the day. `termtrail.com` was registered once before and lapsed (one web-archive capture from April 2024).

## Marks found

None. No identical or closely similar mark turned up for "Termtrail" or "Term Trail" (or เทอมเทรล in Thailand) at any office searched. The nearest results of WIPO's fuzzy search are different words: TERMORAIL, TECHTRAIL, TellTrail, TenoTrail.

No app named Termtrail was found on the Apple App Store (US, Thailand) or Google Play (Thailand).

## What was searched, and what was not

Searched, word-mark text only: Thailand's DIP register (search.ipthailand.go.th), USPTO, EUIPO, IP Australia, and WIPO's Global Brand Database (worldwide, and filtered to Thailand, including fuzzy matching).

Not searched: **TMview** (it refuses automated access; a person can run it at https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/#/tmview/results?page=1&pageSize=30&criteria=C&basicSearch=termtrail), **ASEAN TMview** (unreachable), the **UK** office (human-verification wall), the **Swiss** register (down for maintenance), and Thailand's company registry (DBD). Logos were not compared.

## Practical notes

- Register `termtrail.com` and `termtrail.app` early; both were free on the day.
- "Trail" is easily mistyped as "trial"; consider registering `termtrial.com` as well if it is free.
- Names considered and dropped, with reasons, are in `README.md`.
