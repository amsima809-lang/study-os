# Campus photos (archived)

ARCHIVED 16 November 2026 (owner: "let's get rid of all the background images but save them somewhere"). These were the login page's backgrounds for 12 universities; the page no longer shows any photo, and these files are no longer shipped with the app. They are kept here, with their credits, in case they are wanted again: to bring one back, copy it to `docs/brand/campus-photos/` and restore the photo layer in `client/src/screens/Login.tsx` (git history: commit before "Login: no campus photos").

All come from Wikimedia Commons. Each was cropped to 900×1600 and converted to WebP. When shown, the author and licence must be shown next to the photo.
